An Opportunity to Grow

Behind every good story is a thoughtful team.

Manejah PR is looking for an organized, detail-oriented Marketing & Social Media Coordinator to support the day-to-day execution of client marketing and social media programs.

This is a hands-on, entry-level opportunity for someone who enjoys keeping projects organized, managing details, and ensuring that content is published accurately and on schedule.

You'll gain practical experience supporting a range of client accounts while working across social media, content coordination, digital assets, and the everyday details that keep creative projects moving.

The Opportunity

Marketing & Social Media
Coordinator

A detail-driven role for someone who is dependable, curious, and ready to contribute to the daily rhythm of a creative public relations agency.

Position

Entry level / Intern

Schedule

Immediate start
Approximately 5–10 hours per week

Location

Remote
Boston-based

What You'll Do

  • Schedule and publish social media content across client accounts.
  • Maintain and update monthly content calendars.
  • Organize photography, video, and other digital assets.
  • Coordinate client content reviews, approvals, and revisions.
  • Assist with basic social media analytics and reporting.
  • Support content uploads, administrative tasks, and project coordination.
  • Track deadlines and help keep content deliverables on schedule.
  • Hourly rate based on experience.

Who You Are

  • Highly organized, dependable, and detail-oriented.
  • Familiar with Instagram, LinkedIn, and social media scheduling platforms.
  • Comfortable managing multiple client accounts and deadlines.
  • A strong written communicator.
  • Interested in marketing, public relations, design, and creative businesses.
