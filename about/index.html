A Natural Curiosity

People first.
Stories always.
Strategy in between.

Manejah Terzi is the founder of Manejah PR and someone who has always been driven by curiosity, connection, and a desire to understand what makes people and brands tick.

Positive, intuitive, analytical, and endlessly curious, she has a knack for seeing what makes someone or something special and finding the story worth telling.

With a background in journalism and more than 20 years in public relations and communications, Manejah brings equal parts strategy, creativity, problem-solving, and fun to the work.

She believes the best ideas often come from good conversation, strong relationships, and not taking yourself too seriously.

Through Manejah PR, she works with design-driven businesses and creative professionals to strengthen their voice, build visibility, and communicate in a way that feels thoughtful, relevant, and true to who they are.
